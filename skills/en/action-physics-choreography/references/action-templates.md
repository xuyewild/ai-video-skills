# Action Examples

Each example has two parts: a process-chain passage for writing scripts (`[Action passage]`, remove the marker when writing it back into the script), and beat-by-beat action blocks for writing video prompts (`[Action]` blocks, one block per beat). The characters are fictional. Character and spirit-beast sizes follow your own character sheets. The numbers here are only examples. You can check this file directly with `python3 scripts/validate_action_text.py references/action-templates.md`.

## 1. Swinging a heavy-armored beast into a throw (bare-handed × throw × person-and-beast, clip 10 seconds, 2 beats)

Premise: his superhuman strength is an established setting. The heavy-armored beast stands chest-high to him at the shoulder. Attach a scale reference image of both.

[Action passage]
The heavy-armored beast charges from the right with its head low. He does not retreat. His right foot steps back half a pace into the gravel, and both hands meet the beast and clamp the roots of its two curved horns. The beast's charge pushes him back two steps, and his shoe soles plow two grooves in the ground. He sinks his waist, uses the beast's forward momentum to twist to the left, and pulls the beast's head off line, so both of its front legs leave the ground. He turns on through a half circle, swings the whole beast from the right over his head with both arms, and slams it down toward the front left. The beast's back hits the ground first, a patch of back armor cracks open, and gravel is thrown up to both sides. He lets go of the horns, is carried back one step by the recoil, and stops with one knee down, the hair on his forehead still swaying.

[Action] Swinging a heavy-armored beast into a throw | clip 10s | beat 1/2
Setup: He steps his right foot back half a pace and plants it, and his hands meet the heavy-armored beast charging from the right.
Contact: Both hands clamp the roots of the beast's two curved horns, and its charge pushes him backward two steps while his shoe soles plow two grooves in the ground.
Result: He sinks his waist and twists to the left, pulls the beast's head off line, and both its front legs leave the ground.
Impact: heavy | Environment: the gravel under his feet is pushed apart into two grooves | Shake: the frame dips to the left as he clamps the horns, then returns level
Force direction: the beast charges from frame right to frame left, and he is pushed toward frame left.
Camera: Shot 1, medium shot slightly from below, the camera pulls back fast as the beast charges in; Shot 2, close shot, the camera pushes toward his two hands clamping the horns, and the frame dips to the left as the soles plow the ground; Shot 3, medium shot, the camera holds steady as he pulls the head off line and the front legs lift, with the beast's head and his hands at the center of the frame.

[Action] Swinging a heavy-armored beast into a throw | clip 10s | beat 2/2
Setup: He uses the beast's forward momentum to turn left through a half circle, and both arms swing the beast from the right over his head.
Contact: The beast's back hits the ground first, a patch of back armor cracks open, and gravel is thrown up to both sides.
Result: He lets go of the horns, is carried back one step by the recoil, and stops with one knee down, while the beast lies on its back with its four legs kicking.
Impact: finisher | Environment: the ground is smashed into a shallow pit, gravel is thrown to both sides, and the dust settles last | Shake: the frame jolts downward as the beast's back hits the ground
Force direction: the beast falls downward, from the upper right of the frame to the lower left.
Camera: Shot 1, medium shot, the camera tilts up over his head following the arc of the beast as his arms swing; Shot 2, wide shot, the camera tilts down to the lower left with the beast, the landing spot of its back is the end frame, and the frame jolts downward; Shot 3, medium close shot, the camera pulls back slowly to show him on one knee and the beast on its back, kicking.

Key points: the grip point is a horn that really exists on the beast. He is pushed back first (the beast's weight), then twists (breaks the balance), then swings (the only main force). On landing, the back armor hits first. Seedance 2.0: split each of the two beats into its own 5-second video.

## 2. Whip kick shatters a rock (bare-handed × environment damage, clip 6 seconds, 1 beat)

[Action passage]
He plants his left foot, his waist and hips twist to the left first, and his right leg swings out from the right in an arc. The top of his foot strikes sideways against the side of a waist-high rock. A horizontal crack opens first where the foot touched. Then the upper half slides out to the left and topples, and chips and dust spray toward the left. His right leg carries on through half a circle before it lands, his body turns toward the back left, and the hem of his clothes flies up and drops.

[Action] Whip kick shatters a rock | clip 6s | beat 1/1
Setup: He plants his left foot, his waist and hips twist to the left first, and his right leg swings up from frame right.
Contact: The top of his right foot strikes sideways against the right side of a waist-high rock.
Result: The rock cracks along a horizontal line from the contact point, the upper half slides out toward frame left and topples, and his right leg carries on through half a circle before it lands.
Force direction: a sweep from frame right to the left.
Camera: Shot 1, medium close shot at eye level, the camera pans lightly along the arc to follow his waist twist and the leg swing; Shot 2, close-up, the top of the foot strikes the rock face and the camera pushes quickly toward the crack; Shot 3, medium shot, the camera pulls back as the upper half slides out and topples, with the rock and his leg in the same frame.

Key points: if you want slow motion, mark only the beat from "the top of the foot touches the rock" to "the crack appears". Write `[slow motion] ... [normal speed]` and keep everything else at normal speed. How the debris flies and how long the dust takes to clear is left to effects design.

## 3. Spirit beast pounce (person-and-beast × weapon knocked loose, clip 8 seconds, 2 beats)

Premise: the standing spirit beast is waist-high to the person at the shoulder (take this from the character sheet). Attach a scale reference image. The swordsman holds the sword in his right hand.

[Action passage]
The spirit beast lowers its front body, its hind legs kick two pits in the ground, and its whole body shoots to the left along the ground. In the air its front paws spread, and they press onto the swordsman's chest first. Its weight comes down on him, and the impact throws him backward onto his back. His right elbow slams into the ground first, the sword comes loose from his hand and slides half a zhang (about 1.6 m) along the ground before it stops. The beast's four paws come down, its two front paws still pressing on his chest, and it lowers its head and bares its teeth at his face, the neck fur still standing.

[Action] Spirit beast pounce | clip 8s | beat 1/2
Setup: The spirit beast lowers its front body, pushes off the ground with its hind legs, and pounces toward frame left along the ground.
Contact: Its two front paws press onto the swordsman's chest first.
Result: The swordsman is pressed down and falls backward, and his back lands on the ground.
Force direction: from frame right to the left, pressing downward.
Camera: Shot 1, wide shot, low camera angle close to the ground, the camera tracks to the left with the beast as it shoots out; Shot 2, close shot, a fast push in as the front paws press onto the chest; Shot 3, medium shot, the camera rises slightly as the swordsman falls on his back, with both in the same frame.

[Action] Spirit beast pounce | clip 8s | beat 2/2
Setup: As the swordsman goes down, his right hand still grips the sword and his right elbow hits the ground first.
Contact: The hilt knocks against the ground, and the sword comes loose from his right hand.
Result: The sword slides a short way toward frame left and stops, the beast's four paws land, its front paws still press on his chest, and it lowers its head and bares its teeth.
Force direction: the sword slides toward frame left.
Camera: Shot 1, close shot from above, the elbow hits the ground and the hilt knocks the ground, the camera dips downward; Shot 2, close-up, the camera tilts down to follow the sliding sword; Shot 3, medium close shot, the camera pans back to the beast's front paws and his chest (at the top of the frame).

Key points: the beast's paws press on the clothing and do not sink into the body. The sword leaving the hand is written as "leaves the hand → slides → stops", and after that nobody is holding it.

## 4. Chibi chase (chase × chibi exaggeration, clip 12 seconds, 2 beats)

Premise: upload both the normal-proportion image and the chibi variant image into the same video, and in the prompt write the proportions for each time segment separately.

[Action passage]
He runs into the alley from the right carrying the spirit egg, with three pursuers bunched up behind him. He steps onto a wooden crate at the wall corner and jumps onto the low wall, his left hand pressing on the top of the wall, and the crate is kicked backward and flips over, landing across the alley mouth. [cut to chibi, about 2s] The three pursuers cannot stop. The first one trips on the crate, the other two slam into the back of the one in front in turn, and the three fall forward in a pile, their eyes turned into two circles. [back to normal proportions] He looks back once from the top of the wall, hugs the spirit egg tight, and jumps down to the left.

[Action] Alley chase | clip 12s | beat 1/2
Setup: He runs toward frame left carrying the spirit egg, and his right foot steps onto the wooden crate at the wall corner.
Contact: He pushes off the crate to jump onto the low wall, and his left hand presses on the top of the wall.
Result: The crate is kicked over toward frame right and lands across the alley mouth, and he crouches on top of the wall.
Force direction: he jumps toward the upper left of the frame, and the crate tips toward frame right.
Camera: Shot 1, medium shot, the camera tracks to the left with him as he steps onto the crate; Shot 2, close shot, the camera rises slightly as he jumps onto the low wall and his hand presses on the wall top; Shot 3, medium shot, the crate flips and lands across the alley mouth, the end frame has him on the wall and the crate at the lower right.

[Action] Alley chase | clip 12s | beat 2/2
Setup: [cut to chibi, about 2s] Three chibi pursuers charge in from frame right and cannot stop.
Contact: The first one's foot trips on the crate, and the other two slam into the back of the one in front in turn.
Result: The three fall forward in a pile toward frame left, their eyes turn into two circles, and then [back to normal proportions].
Force direction: the three pitch forward toward frame left.
Camera: Wide shot, fixed camera, the whole frame switches to chibi, with the three at the center of the frame.

Key points: the chibi part does only one thing (a pile-up fall). The exaggeration comes from deformation and pauses, with no extra moves. Proportions return after the cut back. The pursuers move in the same direction as the lead (all to the left), not against each other.

## 5. Two-person weapon duel (weapon × block, path-blocking, guard break, clip 12 seconds, 3 beats)

Premise: Shi Yan attacks and Lin Zhou defends. The story result is that Lin Zhou breaks open Shi Yan's left shoulder armor and Shi Yan kneels. Nobody bleeds.

[Action passage]
Shi Yan charges in from the right and chops down with a two-handed sword stroke from the upper right toward the lower left. Lin Zhou holds his sword flat across his chest and blocks it head-on. The blade smashes into the middle of the sword, and sparks scatter from the contact point. Lin Zhou is pressed back and slides to the left three steps, his boot soles drag two white marks on the flagstones, and his left foot braces against the stone railing behind him before he stops. Shi Yan pulls the blade back, turns, and sweeps across. This stroke does not cut a person. It cuts the wooden rack on Lin Zhou's right. The rack breaks and collapses, sealing Lin Zhou's retreat to the right. Lin Zhou can only move forward. He thrusts his sword straight at Shi Yan's left shoulder armor with both hands, twice into the same seam of the armor. The first thrust knocks off one plate, and the second cracks the whole shoulder piece open and it drops away. Shi Yan staggers two steps to the right and kneels on one knee, the sword tip propped on the flagstones.

[Action] Two-person weapon duel | clip 12s | beat 1/3
Setup: Shi Yan charges in from frame right with both hands holding the sword raised over his right shoulder.
Contact: The blade comes down from the upper right toward the lower left and smashes into the middle of Lin Zhou's sword held across his chest, and sparks scatter from the contact point.
Result: Lin Zhou is pressed back and slides to the left three steps, his boot soles drag two white marks on the flagstones, and his left foot braces against the stone railing behind him and stops.
Impact: heavy | Environment: the boot soles drag white marks on the flagstones, and the stone railing drops a layer of dust when kicked | Shake: the frame dips to the left as the blade lands on the sword, then returns level
Force direction: pressed from the upper right of the frame toward the lower left, and Lin Zhou is pushed toward frame left.
Camera: Shot 1, wide shot, the camera pulls back as Shi Yan charges in; Shot 2, medium shot slightly from below, the camera pushes lightly toward the contact point as the blade falls, with the contact point at the center of the frame; Shot 3, close shot, the boot soles drag white marks and the left foot braces on the railing, the camera tilts down.

[Action] Two-person weapon duel | clip 12s | beat 2/3
Setup: Shi Yan pulls the blade back and turns, and with both hands on the sword he sweeps from the left to the right.
Contact: The blade cuts into the upright post of the wooden rack on Lin Zhou's right.
Result: The rack breaks and falls toward frame left, lying across Lin Zhou's right side and blocking his retreat to the right.
Impact: light | Environment: the rack raises a cloud of dust as it hits the ground, and wood chips roll to Lin Zhou's feet | Shake: none
Force direction: the blade sweeps from frame left to the right, and the rack falls toward frame left.
Camera: Shot 1, wide shot, the camera pans to the right with the blade; Shot 2, medium shot, the camera cuts quickly to the direction of the fall as the rack breaks; Shot 3, medium shot, the end frame holds on the fallen rack and Lin Zhou, with Shi Yan on frame right.

[Action] Two-person weapon duel | clip 12s | beat 3/3
Setup: Lin Zhou's right foot steps forward, and with both hands on the sword he thrusts straight toward frame right.
Contact: The sword tip stabs twice into the same seam of Shi Yan's left shoulder armor, the first knocks off one plate, and the second cracks the whole shoulder piece open so it drops away.
Result: Shi Yan staggers two steps toward frame right and kneels on one knee, the sword tip propped on the flagstones.
Impact: heavy | Environment: the broken armor plates land on the flagstones and bounce away | Shake: the frame dips to the right as the second thrust lands, then returns level
Force direction: from frame left to the right, and Shi Yan is pushed toward frame right.
Camera: Shot 1, medium shot, the camera follows to the right as Lin Zhou steps forward; Shot 2, close shot, the camera pushes quickly toward the shoulder armor as the sword thrusts, with Shi Yan's shoulder armor as the frame subject and only the sword and hand of Lin Zhou left in the foreground; Shot 3, medium close shot, he steadies after the second thrust lands, the plates drop to the ground, and Shi Yan staggers and kneels.

Key points: beat 1 shows weight through the sliding distance and the marks from the boot soles. Beat 2 does not hit a person. It hits the environment to block the path, so it is light contact with no shake, which gives the rhythm a breath. Beat 3 hits the same spot repeatedly, each deeper than the last. It is a guard break, not random stabbing. Strength is shown by the difference in displacement: Lin Zhou backs off three steps and stops, Shi Yan backs off two steps and kneels. There is no blood in the whole sequence. The damage is shown by the armor plates.

## Counter-example table

| Don't write it like this | Write it like this |
|---|---|
| He kicks violently and smashes the rock to pieces fiercely | His left foot is planted, the top of his right foot strikes sideways against the right side of the rock, and the upper half slides out to the left and topples |
| The two fight intensely for dozens of rounds | One beat has one pair of contact. Split dozens of rounds into several beats, or carry them with a whip pan |
| The spirit beast leaps and the enemy is knocked flying | Front paws press on the chest first → falls on his back → his back hits the ground |
| A flash of blade light and the enemy falls | The blade chops diagonally from the upper right of the frame toward the lower left, hits the left shoulder armor, and he staggers two steps toward the back left and kneels |
| The sword qi hits and he is blown away | The sword qi smashes into the protective qi shield first, the shield shatters into green wind fragments, and he slides back five steps, his boot soles plowing two grooves |
| The two collide and each steps back one pace | Shi Yan backs off five steps in a row, and Lin Zhou slides back three steps and stops (the displacement difference shows strength) |
| The two keep exchanging blows, each faster than the last | Each beat has one pair of contact, one beat hits the environment to block the path, one beat has no shake, and the rhythm rises and falls |
| He lands from the air | His right knee touches down first and smashes a ring of cracks, he slides forward a foot before he steadies, and the cloth at the knee is worn through |
| One kick sends him flying far away | His left knee lifts and holds for half a beat, a twisting side kick lands square on the belly; his body folds in half, both feet leave the ground, he flies backward three zhang (about 10 m), breaks a bamboo fence, and rolls once on landing; after he lowers the leg and plants it, the hem of his clothes drops last |
| He dodges quickly | The sword tip goes straight for the throat, his upper body leans back with his weight on the right rear foot, the tip passes close along his chin and cuts off a lock of hanging hair; he uses the momentum to twist to the left and gives up half a step |
| He uses his ultimate move and the opponent falls | He draws the sword back to his waist, the wind stops suddenly, and his robe hangs still for a beat; he steps forward and slashes across, and the whole row of bamboo behind him is cut at waist height and falls half a beat late; he stays frozen at the end point of the stroke |
| The two team up in perfect sync | She bends down and sweeps a leg to force the opponent to jump, he steps on her shoulder and rises, and at the opponent's highest point he slaps a palm onto the chest; the opponent's back slams into the ground, and she has already rolled aside from the landing spot |
